## Full Description (4000 chars max)
```
BabyCam.io turns your Android devices into a professional-grade baby monitoring system with enterprise-level live video streaming.

📹 HD & 4K STREAMING
Stream in up to 4K Ultra HD when bandwidth allows. Adaptive bitrate automatically adjusts quality — so you never lose your connection, even on slower networks.

🔊 TWO-WAY AUDIO
Talk back to soothe your baby with crystal-clear audio, optimized to work reliably even on low-end devices.

🌐 MULTI-NETWORK SUPPORT
Seamlessly switches between Wi-Fi, mobile data (2G/3G/4G/5G), and local networks. Works anywhere, anytime.

📡 AUDIO FALLBACK
If bandwidth drops too low for video, BabyCam.io automatically falls back to audio-only mode — so you're never disconnected.

🔒 END-TO-END SECURITY
Encrypted video rooms with token-based access. Only the people you authorize can view your stream. Bank-grade security for your family's privacy.

🔔 SMART ALERTS
AI-powered cry detection and motion alerts sent instantly to all connected devices. Know the moment something needs your attention.

👥 MULTI-VIEWER ACCESS (Pro)
Share secure access with your partner, grandparents, or babysitter. Everyone stays connected.

💰 FLEXIBLE PLANS
• Free: 1 room, 720p streaming, basic alerts — completely free with ads
• Pro ($9.97/mo or $89/yr): Unlimited rooms, 4K streaming, no ads, AI cry detection, two-way audio, priority 24/7 support

🌍 AVAILABLE WORLDWIDE
Works in 190+ countries with multiple payment options: Credit/Debit Card, PayPal, GCash/Maya (Philippines), M-Pesa (Kenya & Africa).

🗣️ 13 LANGUAGES
Available in English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Swahili, Tagalog, and Chinese.

🛠️ BONUS TOOLS
Built-in unit converter (length, weight, temperature, volume) and spirit level tool — handy utilities for parents and around the house.

🤖 AI ASSISTANT
Get instant answers about baby care, measurements, and more with the built-in AI assistant.

⚠️ IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER
BabyCam.io is a monitoring tool and is NOT a substitute for in-person supervision of children. Always maintain appropriate physical supervision.

Download BabyCam.io today and start monitoring in minutes — no extra hardware required.
```
